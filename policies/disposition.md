# Disposition policy

## Machine-readable settings

fraud_action: block_card
ambiguous_action: request_manual_review

Only recommend irreversible action after the account has been assessed and, where required, approved by a human reviewer.

- Fraud cases may require card blocking and escalation.
- Legitimate cases should be released.
- Ambiguous cases should be sent to manual review rather than forced into fraud or legitimate.
- Human approval is required before any irreversible action.

Use customer notes, prior cases, and disputes to determine whether the alert is already explained.

- Prefer explicit explanatory notes over rule triggers.
- A confirmed phone upgrade, verified KYC, or shared household device history is usually legitimate evidence.
- Lack of explanatory context means the account may be insufficiently evidenced rather than automatically fraudulent.
